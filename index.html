About

A great product built with imperfect tech beats a poor product built with perfect tech.

I have built software for over 15 years. I follow the product, not a stack, and learn whatever it takes to build it well. That path led me through a digital agency, a software house building startup MVPs, and product companies in streaming and digital health.

In every job, I improve how teams build. I brought Ruby to a PHP team, took a frontend from zero tests to 80% coverage, and led migrations to TypeScript.

Today I focus on AI. I build LLM features into products, and I build Claude Code workflows that take my team from ticket to a tested pull request. I also enjoy mentoring engineers and working closely with design and business.
